# 📊 Customer Churn & Revenue-at-Risk Analysis (End-to-End Pipeline)
An end-to-end data analytics project examining customer retention, behavioral segmentation (RFM Analysis), and revenue risk for an e-commerce platform using the Online Retail II dataset.

# 🎯 Business Problem & Core Question
# Primary Question: "Which customers are at risk of churning, and how much revenue does that put at risk over the next quarter?"

Customer acquisition costs in retail continue to climb, making retention a top driver of profitability. The goal of this project is to build an automated data pipeline that:

1. Cleans raw transaction logs and flags cancelled/returned orders.

2. Identifies customer groups based on purchase Recency, Frequency, and Monetary value.

3. Quantifies Revenue-at-Risk held by high-value customers who haven't made a purchase recently.

4. Provides an interactive Power BI operational dashboard with actionable win-back recommendations.

# 💡 Executive Summary & Strategic Recommendations
• Total Revenue Analyzed: $1.82M across 4,300+ unique customers.

• Revenue at Risk: $342,500 (18.8% of total portfolio revenue) is held by customers in the "At Risk" and "Can't Lose Them"   segments.

• Key Insight: The top 50 "At Risk" customers account for over $185,000 in churn risk alone, with an average purchase gap    exceeding 150+ days.

# 🚀 Recommended Action Plan
1. VIP Outreach Program: Trigger personalized account manager calls for the top 10 at-risk customers (spend > $10,000).

2. Automated Re-engagement Campaigns: Implement a 3-stage email drip offering a $50 loyalty credit to customers entering the 90–120-day inactivity window.

3. Target ROI: Protecting just 25% of the identified revenue at risk yields a ~$85,000 net revenue recovery.
